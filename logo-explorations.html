Wordmark typefaces

Six heavy grotesks, condensed to extended, chosen from a long list of 24 (rejected outright: Anybody and Saira for looking like games, Antonio and League Gothic for being generic, Bebas, Oswald and Space Grotesk for being AI and fitness clichés). Every sample is outlined, not live text, and spaced by hand: an optical-gap pass measures the white space between each pair on 120 scanlines, then each pair is judged by eye at 24px and 200px. FORMGUARD in capitals reads as equipment. FormGuard in camel case reads as an app. Recommendation: capitals.

An engineered grotesk with squared curves (super-ellipse O, D, G, U). It reads like the markings on a calibrated instrument: machined, calm, heavy without shouting. Distinctive without being decorative.

Spacing · Hubot Sans

Font default (top) · hand-kerned, +45 tracking (bottom)

The default spacing is uneven: FO gapes and UA pinches. The largest-to-smallest optical gap spread falls from 6.2% to 0.2% of cap height. Pair adjustments, in units of 1/1000 em:

Level line · Hubot Sans

F arm, R bowl and G bar share one height

The F's middle arm (421.0) and G's bar (402.5) are moved onto the R's bowl join (406.5), so the word holds one exact level, the way a lifter holds a bar. You don't see it, you feel it. Keep for the final wordmark.

Modified G · Hubot Sans

Hubot's spurred G (left) · spurless G built from its own O (right)

The spurless G is drawn from Hubot's own O bowl, opened at the terminal height (477) and given the same bar, so there's no seam. It's smoother but softer and more generic, like any geometric sans. The square spur under the bar is what makes the word feel machined. Keep the spur. The G's only change is the 4-unit raise onto the level line.

A cut at parallel · Hubot Sans

One horizontal cut through the word at 47% of cap height

The depth line as a cut. It looks like sportswear and like a stencil, and at small sizes it closes up and adds noise. It's an effect, not an idea. Rejected.

Spacing · Pathway Extreme

Font default (top) · hand-kerned, +40 tracking (bottom)

At opsz 72 the defaults are display-tight. They're good for a headline but too tight for a logo that also has to work at 14px. Opened by 4% of cap height, with FO closed −22 and MG opened +8.

Joined pair · Pathway Extreme

U and A locked together

Locking the U into the A's diagonal makes a sports-club ligature. It knots up the middle of GUARD and has nothing to do with form or guarding. Rejected.
