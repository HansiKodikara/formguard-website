# FormGuard brand rules

The identity is **A · Rack F**, chosen from `logo-explorations.html`. The F is a power rack seen side-on: upright, J-hook, safety arm. It holds the bar, in Plate Red, in a socket. In the horizontal lockup that F is also the first letter of FORMGUARD.

All artwork lives in `/brand` and comes from `logo/tools/export_brand.py`, which reuses the refined geometry. **Never redraw, retype, re-space or "improve" the logo.** Always use the files. On the website, every logo goes through the one `<Logo>` component (`src/components/Logo.tsx`). It reads the same path data (`src/brand/logo-data.ts`, generated by the export script).

## Artwork

| Asset | Files (`brand/svg/`, PNG in `brand/png/` at 1x/2x) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup (drop-cap) | `formguard-lockup-{primary,reversed,iron,chalk}.svg` | Default. Headers, footers, documents. |
| Symbol, master | `formguard-symbol-{…}.svg` | Avatars, the suit's chest, app icons. **48px and up.** |
| Symbol, 32px cut | `formguard-symbol-32-{…}.svg` | 32–47px. Pixel-drawn: stem 6, arms 5, bar ⌀8. |
| Symbol, 16px cut | `formguard-symbol-16-{…}.svg` | 16–31px. Pixel-drawn: stem 3, arms 2, bar ⌀4. |
| Stacked lockup | `formguard-stacked-{…}.svg` | Square-ish spaces: posters, splash screens. |
| Favicons | `brand/favicon/`: `favicon.svg` (32px cut, Iron on light tabs, Chalk on dark), `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-16.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `icon-512.png` | Site head, PWA. |
| Open Graph | `brand/og/og-image.png` (1200×630) | Link previews. Rendered from the `<Logo>` component. |

Colourways:
- `primary`: Iron Black ink with a Plate Red bar, for light backgrounds.
- `reversed`: Chalk ink with a Plate Red bar, for dark backgrounds.
- `iron` and `chalk`: one colour, for single-colour print, embroidery, engraving and the suit.

## Size: master above 48px, pixel cuts below

The master symbol's socket and gaps are precise. Below 48px they close up, so smaller sizes use the pixel-drawn cuts, which put stems on whole pixels:

| Rendered symbol height | Use |
|---|---|
| 48px and up | master |
| 32–47px | 32px cut |
| 16–31px | 16px cut |
| under 16px | don't |

Minimum sizes:
- **Horizontal lockup:** 24px tall on screen (about 190px wide) or 8mm tall in print. It is 7.9× as wide as it is tall.
- **Stacked lockup:** 72px tall on screen, 20mm in print.
- **Symbol:** 16px, using the 16px cut.

## Clear space

The unit is the bar's diameter, which is **0.32 × the symbol's height** (in the lockup, measured on the drop-cap F). Keep at least one unit clear on every side of every lockup and the symbol. Nothing else (type, edges, other logos) enters it.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Black | `#1B1A17` | Logo ink on light, text, dark "gym" sections |
| Chalk | `#F2EDE4` | Logo ink on dark, light sections |
| Plate Red | `#C62B22` | **Only the bar** in the logo. On the site: the brand accent and the colour of *flagged* reps |

The functional good-rep colour and the neutral greys are defined in the website's design system (`src/styles/tokens.css`). They are never used in the logo.

- Plate Red is **only ever the bar**. The F, the word and the clear space are never red. No version of the logo is all red.
- Logo backgrounds are flat Chalk or Iron Black, or a calm, dark photographic area. Never place it over busy imagery without a solid field.

## Typefaces

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Hubot Sans 760, width 110 | Outlined in the artwork; never set the logo in live type |
| Headlines | Hubot Sans 700–800, width 100–110 | Heavy and wide, used big. Its zero is barred, so set big numerals in Mona Sans |
| Body | Mona Sans 400–600 | Hubot's sibling, with a plain zero |
| Data readouts | IBM Plex Mono 400–600 | Capitals, tracked: `REP 07 · KNEE VALGUS · L · 12°` |

All three are OFL. The site self-hosts subset builds renamed "FG Display", "FG Text" and "FG Mono", because the OFL's Reserved Font Names ("Hubot", "Mona", "Plex") can't be used on modified (subset) files. Licences are in `public/fonts/`.

## Don't

- Don't redraw, re-kern, retype or re-space anything, and don't separate the drop-cap F from ORMGUARD.
- Don't put the symbol next to the horizontal lockup ("F FORMGUARD"). Use one or the other.
- Don't use the master symbol below 48px; use the cuts.
- Don't recolour: red is only the bar, and one-colour versions are Iron or Chalk.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows, glows, gradients or textures to the logo.
- Don't put the logo in a container shape. The app icon's square is the only exception.
